Interpretation layer

What is actually being simulated?

EXACT NUMERICAL MODEL

Statevector engine

The browser stores complex amplitudes and applies finite-dimensional unitary matrices. For 1–6 systems it tracks every amplitude explicitly, so probabilities are calculated as $p(x)=|a_x|^2$ up to floating-point error.

CHECKED AGAINST REFERENCES

Parity evidence

Selected circuits have been compared with the native NumPy simulator and Qiskit Statevector. Matching amplitudes and probabilities show implementation agreement, not proof that the browser is connected to physical qubits.

RESTRICTED SCALING

Sparse 50 mode

For 7–50 systems the browser stores only nonzero basis amplitudes. This is deterministic numerical evolution for sparse-preserving circuits while support stays bounded. It is not an arbitrary dense 50-qubit simulator.

NUMERICAL LINEAR ALGEBRA

Polar transform

The golden-phase dictionary is a deterministic matrix. Its Gram matrix measures column overlap; multiplying by the inverse square root produces the unitary polar/Löwdin basis. This is a calculation, not a new physical law.

EXPERIMENTAL MODEL

Vertex geometry

The tetrahedron is an inspectable graph model inspired by geometric-storage ideas in the paper. Braiding updates phase, holonomy, and diagnostic invariants. It is not a demonstrated topological qubit, hardware device, or fault-tolerant code.

NOT CLAIMED

What this does not prove

Nothing here establishes quantum advantage, physical quantum computation, arbitrary 50-qubit simulation, efficient asymptotic synthesis, cryptographic security, or superiority over the standard QFT.

Rule of interpretation:visuals explain data produced by the model; they do not upgrade a classical simulation into a physical quantum experiment.
