Shared foundations

Different engines.
One state space.

Anharmonic Labs calculates directly in the finite complex Hilbert space used by statevector simulators. That shared mathematics—not shared source code—is why independent engines converge on the same state.

State evolution
|ψout⟩ = U |ψin⟩

A normalized state is evolved by a unitary operator. Measurement probabilities are the squared magnitudes of its coordinates.
